Design Task: Six-station inspection conveyor, 60 unique mechanical parts

Build a code-driven, editable CAD assembly with six independently identifiable
stations. Each station has ten different functional parts, for exactly 60 solid
parts total. This is a test of distinct geometry, sub-assemblies, interfaces,
assembly checks, BOMs, and independently reopened STEP exports. Do not replace
the parts with repeated blocks, a fused monolith, or colored mesh placeholders.

Use millimetres. For station index i = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, place its shaft axis at
x = 50 + 100i, z = 28, parallel to Y. The following ten parts belong to each
station and must have their own part IDs and different source geometry:

1. Aluminium base plate, length 78 + 2i, width 82, thickness 6, with four
   through mounting holes diameter 5.5 on a 58 x 62 rectangular pattern.
2. Left aluminium bearing support, 18 x 16 x (25 + i), with a coaxial diameter
   10.05 through bore and two mounting holes. Its bore axis is the shaft axis.
3. Right aluminium bearing support, 18 x 18 x (25 + i), with a coaxial diameter
   10.05 through bore and a distinct relieved outside face.
4. Steel roller shaft, diameter 10.00 and overall length 76 + i, with two
   diameter 8.00 end journals and one flat on its right end.
5. Aluminium roller sleeve, outside diameter 24 + 0.5i, length 36 + i, with a
   diameter 10.10 through bore, two 2 mm edge chamfers, and three axial grooves.
6. Aluminium drive pulley, outside diameter 30 + i, width 8, with a diameter
   10.10 bore, a shallow belt groove, and a flat matching the shaft end.
7. Steel spacer, outside diameter 16 + 0.4i, width 4 + 0.2i, with a diameter
   10.10 bore and a 1 mm outside chamfer.
8. Aluminium sensor bracket, L profile with 4 mm wall, 20 + i horizontal leg,
   26 + i vertical leg, one diameter 6.5 sensor hole and one slotted mount.
9. Aluminium folded guard, 2 mm wall, with two flanges, radiused bends, and a
   window over the roller. Vary window length by 1 mm per station.
10. Aluminium tensioner block, 14 x (20 + i) x 12, with a 6 x (10 + i) slot,
    a diameter 5.5 mounting hole, and a filleted outside edge.

Use clear, non-overlapping placement for all non-mated parts. Put the two bearing
supports on opposite ends of each roller shaft and fit the roller sleeve
between them. Put the pulley outside the right support and the spacer outside
the left support. Position the sensor bracket, guard, and tensioner above or
beside their station without interference. State any placement or fit clearance
you add and why. Do not change the specified dimensions silently.

Deliver one STEP assembly with all 60 solids, a structured BOM with 60 unique
part rows of quantity one, a component inventory, and a short measurement report.
Independently reopen the STEP and report its solid count, overall envelope
(required <= 650 x 140 x 120), six measured shaft-axis separations (target
100.0 +/- 0.1 between adjacent stations), bearing-axis alignment (<= 0.05),
selected-pair clearances, and all detected unintended interferences. Report
mass using aluminium 2.70 g/cm3 and steel 7.85 g/cm3, flagging any missing
density. Mark physical wear, vibration, and belt performance unverified.
