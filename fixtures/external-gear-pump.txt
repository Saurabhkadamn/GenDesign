Design Task: External Gear Pump (3-piece, cast aluminium, ~8.5 cc/rev)

## Objective
A hydraulic external spur gear pump: front cover, gear housing, rear cover,
two gear-shafts, bushings, seal and fasteners. Tests cavity cutting,
specified-distance meshing, three tolerance chains, standard parts, cast DFM
and calculation-vs-geometry consistency.

## Key Dimensions (use exactly; state anything you add)
* Gears: module 2.0, 17 teeth, 20 deg pressure angle, no profile shift,
  true involute teeth. Face width 20.00 (-0.010 / -0.020).
  Tip dia 38.00 (-0.030 / -0.050), root dia 29.0.
* Shaft centre distance 34.000 +/-0.010.
* Gear housing: figure-8 THROUGH bore, two dia 38.10 (+0.025 / 0) bores on the
  34.000 centres. Housing thickness 20.06 +/-0.010.
* Shaft journals dia 16 (15.966 to 15.984). Bushings ID 16.000 to 16.018, OD 20.
* Ports: inlet and outlet G 1/2 (ISO 228) on opposite sides at the mesh plane.

## Components (~27 instances)
Gear housing (1, die-cast Al), front cover (1, with seal bore), rear cover (1),
drive gear-shaft (1, dia 16 x 30 stub with keyway), driven gear-shaft (1),
bushings (4), lip seal 16x28x7 (1), O-rings ISO 3601 (2), M8x50 socket head
screws ISO 4762 (6) + washers (6), dowel pins ISO 2338 dia 6 (2), key DIN 6885
5x5x20 (1).

## Assembly Requirements
1. Both gears sit inside the figure-8 cavity with zero overlap with housing
   material. Report the radial gear-to-bore clearance measured from the built model.
2. Each shaft axis concentric with its two bushing bores <=0.05 mm; shaft axes
   parallel; axis separation 34.000 +/-0.010. Report measured values.
3. Export three states with the drive gear turned 0, 5.29 and 10.59 deg.
   Zero interference between teeth and between gears and housing, covers and seal
   in all three. Report circumferential backlash (target 0.08 to 0.20 mm) and how
   it is achieved. If tooth form is simplified, say so.
4. Tolerance chains, each as nominal / worst-case min / max / pass or fail:
   (a) radial tip clearance, gear tip to bore: required 0.04 to 0.12 mm.
   (b) axial clearance, housing thickness minus gear width: required 0.04 to 0.08 mm.
   (c) journal-to-bushing diametral clearance: required 0.015 to 0.060 mm.
   If a chain fails anywhere in its range, say so plainly and name which tolerance
   to change. Do not report only the nominal.
5. Calculations with formulas shown: displacement (cc/rev) and the method used,
   contact ratio (>=1.2), flow at 1500 rpm and 92% volumetric efficiency, drive
   torque at 150 bar, hydraulic power. Then a table comparing calculated gear
   dimensions (pitch, tip, root dia, centre distance, face width) with MEASURED
   values from the model.
6. Standard parts: state exact designations and dimensions (G 1/2 thread major
   and pitch, lip seal, O-ring size, ISO 4762, ISO 2338, DIN 6885).
   Do not invent non-standard sizes.
7. DFM: housing and covers are die-cast aluminium: draft >=1 deg, fillets >=2 mm,
   min wall >=4 mm. Report measured values, state the parting line, flag anything
   non-manufacturable.
8. Integrity: zero interference across the whole assembly in all three states,
   except intentional press fits (bushings, dowels), each named. No duplicate or
   redundant parts. Report final solid count against the list above and
   explain any extra. Every part independently identifiable.
9. Envelope <=130 x 90 x 130 mm. Total mass reported, density-unassigned flagged.

## Final Requirement
Deliver the STEP for all three states, the calculation notebook, the measured-vs-calculated table and all three chain reports. Flag sealing under
pressure, pressure ripple, wear, cavitation and efficiency as unverified.
